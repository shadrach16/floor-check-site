Delete your account and data

Effective: 2026-10-28

This page explains how to delete your Floor Check account and the data linked to it. Floor Check is made by Wao App Studios (BN 9804636), Lagos, Nigeria, trading as Retinue. Contact: support.retinue@gmail.com.

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Floor Check.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted from our server straight away, and the app's data on this device is cleared.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email support.retinue@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address linked to your account (for example the Google account you signed in with), or include enough detail for us to find it. We will confirm the request is yours, delete the account and its data, and reply to confirm, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted, and what we keep

Deleted

  • Your account (and your name, email and picture, if you signed in with Google)
  • Everything you created or saved in the app [list your app's data types here]
  • Your settings and preferences
  • Your device notification token, if you allowed notifications

Kept, and why

  • Minimal purchase records (for example an order id and date) where the law requires them for tax, accounting or fraud prevention, usually for up to 7 years. They do not include your app content.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop future charges, cancel it in the Play Store app: Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Data only on your device

Anything the app keeps only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Floor Check → Storage → Clear data.

Deletion cannot be undone.
