Floor Check

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-07

This policy explains what Floor Check (the "app", Android package com.retinue.floorcheck) does with your information. The app is made by Wao App Studios (BN 9804636), Lagos, Nigeria, trading as Retinue ("we", "us"). Contact: support.retinue@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • Floor Check has no account and no login. We do not collect your trips or your pay.
  • The trip files, pay statements, screenshots and numbers you open or type stay on your phone.
  • No analytics SDK, no ads, no advertising ID, no location, no contacts.
  • Apart from Google Play purchases (section 5), the one thing that can leave your phone is a creator's code, if you came from a creator's link or typed one (section 6).
  • We never sell or share your information.

2. What stays on your phone

Your settings (where you drive, your pay period), the trips and pay periods you check, the column mappings you confirm and a few simple counters (for example the date you first opened the app and how many files you imported) are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Android backup is turned off for the app, so this data is not copied to a cloud backup. "Delete all data" in Settings removes it; uninstalling the app removes it too.

3. Files you open and things you share

When you open a file, the app reads it on your phone through Android's file picker or share sheet. The app never uploads it. If you choose to share something from the app (a Field check, the Diagnostics counters, an export of your data or a claim pack PDF), Android's share sheet sends it only where you choose.

4. The rate table

To keep the published rates current, the app may download a public rate file from our static web host. The request carries no information about you, no identifier and no trip data. The host's ordinary server logs (such as the IP address and time of a request) are kept by the host, and we do not use them.

5. Purchases

If you start the Full Audit subscription, the payment is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. The app checks with Google Play on your phone whether the subscription is active. To do that the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service that works for us: it receives your Google Play purchase record and a random app user number made on your phone (not your name, email address or an advertising ID), and tells the app whether Full Audit is active (RevenueCat's privacy policy). We never see or store your card details.

6. Creator codes

Floor Check pays creators who recommend it a share of the subscriptions their followers buy. If you installed the app from a creator's link, or you type a creator's code on the Full Audit screen, the app keeps that code on your phone and does three things with it, and nothing else:

  • It asks our creator program service whether the code exists and whether its 30-day free trial is open. The request carries the code and the app's name, nothing about you or your trips.
  • Once per install, it tells that service that the code brought an install: the code, how it arrived (a link or typed), the app's name and a random install number made on your phone for this purpose. It is not your phone's ID or an advertising ID, and the service stores it only in a scrambled (hashed) form.
  • In a version of the app that sells Full Audit, the code is also stored with RevenueCat, the service that tells the app whether your subscription is active, as soon as the app starts (whether or not you subscribe), so that the creator can be paid their share if you do.

Like any web request, these reach the service with your network address; the service stores only a scrambled form that changes every day, to stop abuse, and we do not use the web server's ordinary logs. A code never unlocks anything by itself: Full Audit comes only from a Google Play purchase. "Delete all data" in Settings removes the code from your phone and from RevenueCat. Without a creator code, none of this happens.

7. What we do not do

No analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, no advertising ID, no location, no access to your contacts, no account, and no selling of personal data. Floor Check never contacts a rideshare platform or a government agency for you, and never files anything.

8. Children

Floor Check is made for adult drivers and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Your rights

We hold no account and nothing that tells us who you are: your data is on your phone and under your control. A creator code is removed with "Delete all data" (section 6). Questions: support.retinue@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts sending any other information anywhere, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety form before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
