The rule

For each pay period, your net earnings, not counting tips, must reach at least:

  • 120% of the applicable minimum wage for every engaged hour, plus
  • a per-mile amount for every engaged mile ($0.30 in 2021, raised each year with inflation).

Engaged time runs from accepting a request to completing it. A shortfall is paid with the next period.

Being checked

This year's per-mile amount, which minimum wage applies (the state's, or a higher local one where you pick up) and where to complain are being checked on the State Treasurer's and the Department of Industrial Relations' own pages. No figure on this page is final until then.

What the app does with your pay statements

Open your weekly pay statements or screenshots in Floor Check. It adds up each pay period's engaged hours and miles and shows the period green, red or amber, with the rule on the card. California checks switch on once this year's figures are confirmed; until then the app shows no red for California. Nothing leaves your phone. No login.
