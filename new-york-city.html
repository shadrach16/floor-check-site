The rule

For each trip, the app company must pay you at least the per-mile rate times the trip miles plus the per-minute rate times the trip minutes.

Wheelchair-accessible vehicles get a higher per-mile rate. Trips that start in the city and end outside it get the out-of-town rates. The rates change every 1 March.

To complain: email the TLC at DriverProtection@tlc.nyc.gov.

What the app does with your trips

New York City does not require a receipt file, so Floor Check reads your trip screenshots or the minutes, miles and pay you type. Every trip turns green, red or amber against the TLC rate for its date, with the rule on each card. Nothing leaves your phone. No login.
